# Limits & risks: what this radar cannot do

- The median held-out miss is about 2.6x: treat every implied value as an order of magnitude, not a price. Held-out R² sits near or below zero (random split −0.94, leave-one-category-out 0.10, leave-one-out 0.00) because one or two protocols carry market caps that ignore TVL entirely.
- Launch odds are calibrated on 148 held-out protocols (five-fold Brier 0.201): 21% means about one in five similar protocols launched, not that this one will.
- Farm costs are estimates: capital bands of $50–$250, gas at $2–$8 a step, weekly gas over the hold. Real gas and slippage vary by chain and day.
- The snapshot is one day, 2026-11-14. TVL, growth and listings move after it; the board does not.
- A protocol the snapshot records as tokenless may still have a token its record misses. "No token recorded" is not proof of "no token".
- Nothing here is financial advice. Farm only what you can afford to lose.
