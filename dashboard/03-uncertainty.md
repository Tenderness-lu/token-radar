# How wide is each value? The uncertainty band

Every implied value carries a band, in the Range column: a low and a high
beside the point estimate. The band is the middle half of the model's
leave-one-out misses: on the 87 fitted protocols, the signed LOO residuals
(actual minus predicted, log terms) run from -0.95 (25th percentile) to +1.27
(75th), so a fitted value is multiplied by 0.39 at the low end and 3.56 at
the high end. Sentora Curator at $52.4M reads $20.3M–$186.9M.

The band is the same proportional width on every row, because the model's
relative error does not depend on the protocol. It is a statement about the
model, not the protocol: half of similar protocols land outside it, and the
median miss is still about 2.6x. Treat the point estimate as the middle of a
wide range, not a price.
